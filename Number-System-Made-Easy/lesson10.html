Decimal and Hexadecimal

Practise converting numbers between decimal and hexadecimal.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

To convert decimal to hexadecimal, divide repeatedly by 16 and read remainders upward. To convert hexadecimal to decimal, multiply each digit by its power of 16 and add.

WORKED EXAMPLE

45₁₀ = 2×16 + 13 = 2D₁₆, because D represents 13.
