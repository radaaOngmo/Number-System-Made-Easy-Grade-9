Binary to Octal

Convert binary to octal by grouping bits in threes.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Group binary digits in sets of three starting from the right. Add leading zeros when needed, then convert each group to an octal digit.

WORKED EXAMPLE

110101₂ → 110 101 → 6 5 → 65₈.
