Decimal to Binary

Convert decimal integers to binary using repeated division.

LEARN THE CONCEPT

Key idea

Divide the decimal number repeatedly by two and record the remainders. Read the remainders from bottom to top.

WORKED EXAMPLE

13 ÷ 2 = 6 r1; 6 ÷ 2 = 3 r0; 3 ÷ 2 = 1 r1; 1 ÷ 2 = 0 r1. So 13₁₀ = 1101₂.
